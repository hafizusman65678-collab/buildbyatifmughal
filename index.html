About MedGuide Offline

MedGuide Offline is a self-contained educational clinical reference tool designed for learning and quick reference. It runs entirely in your browser with no internet connection, external APIs, or tracking.

What this tool is

  • An educational disease and medicine reference
  • A structured symptom explorer that shows possible educational matches based on symptom overlap
  • A local drug information lookup covering class, mechanism, indications, cautions, and counseling points

What this tool is not

  • Not a diagnostic device or medical device
  • Not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment
  • Not a prescribing tool — it never recommends a specific medicine for an individual patient
  • Not an emergency service

Safety principles

  • Symptom matching is clearly labeled as educational only
  • Emergency warning signs are highlighted when relevant
  • Medicine information includes contraindications, interactions, and monitoring points
  • Language avoids “take this drug” instructions

How to use offline

  1. Extract the ZIP file to any folder on your computer or device
  2. Open index.html in a modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge, Safari, etc.)
  3. No installation, server, or internet connection is required

Adding more data

Disease entries live in data/diseases.js. Medicine entries live in data/drugs.js. Follow the existing object structure and reload the page after saving changes.

Privacy

All data stays on your device. Theme preference is stored in local browser storage only. No analytics, accounts, or network requests are made by this application.
